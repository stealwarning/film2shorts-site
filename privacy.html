Privacy Policy — film2shorts

1. Data we collect

TikTok account info (username, nickname) via Login Kit. OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) needed to upload videos. Video files the owner chooses to publish.

2. How we use it

Solely to log in and publish videos to the owner's own TikTok account. Nothing is shared, sold, or sent to third parties. No analytics, no ads.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored on the owner's private home server and never leave it except for calls to the official TikTok API.

4. Your rights

Revoke access anytime via TikTok Settings > Security > Manage app permissions. Contact us to delete any stored data.
