film2shorts

film2shorts is a personal automation tool by an independent creator. It cuts long videos into short vertical episodes and publishes them to the owner's own TikTok account via the official TikTok API.

Features

  • Automatic cutting of films into short episodes (vertical 1080x1920).
  • Scheduled publishing to the owner's TikTok account.
  • Private home-server setup, single user, no public sign-ups.
